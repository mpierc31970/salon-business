## Roles

Your site collects personal data from your customers, such as names, email addresses and form messages. For that data, you decide what is collected and why. Superior Salon processes it on your behalf and only on your instructions, which are the settings you choose in your dashboard.

## What Superior Salon does with the data

Superior Salon stores, shows and sends the data only to run your site and the add-ons you install. Superior Salon does not sell it, does not use it for advertising and does not combine it with other customers' data.

## Security

Superior Salon keeps your data on servers it controls, sends it over encrypted connections, limits who at Superior Salon can see it, and logs every time Superior Salon staff open your dashboard. Superior Salon backs the data up daily.

## Deleting data

You can delete your customers' records from your dashboard at any time. If a customer asks you to delete their data and you can't do it yourself, email [matthew@metaphysicalevents.com](mailto:matthew@metaphysicalevents.com) and Superior Salon will do it.

## Problems

If Superior Salon learns that your customers' data has been lost or seen by someone who should not have it, Superior Salon tells you without undue delay, with what it knows and what it is doing about it.
