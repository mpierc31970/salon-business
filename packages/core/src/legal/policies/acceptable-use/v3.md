## Why this policy exists

Superior Salon hosts every site on one shared platform, so one site can put every other customer at risk. This policy says which sites Superior Salon hosts, which it reviews first, and what happens when a site breaks the rules. The same lists are shown on Superior Salon's website and in setup before you pay.

## Sites we don't host

<!-- clause:aup-never-hosted -->
Superior Salon does not host sites of these types, whatever the business calls itself:

- **Illegal content or activity:** Anything illegal where Superior Salon or the business operates
- **Adult sexual content and services:** Pornography, escort services, adult live chat
- **Weapons:** Selling firearms, ammunition, explosives or weapon parts
- **Drugs:** Illegal drugs, drug paraphernalia, unapproved pharmaceuticals
- **Gambling:** Online casinos, betting, lotteries
- **Hate, harassment, violence:** Content attacking people for who they are, threats, extremism
- **Fraud and deception:** Scams, phishing, fake reviews, impersonation, get-rich-quick schemes, pyramid schemes
- **Counterfeit and infringement:** Counterfeit goods, pirated media, others' copyrighted work without permission
- **Malware and abuse:** Malware, spam operations, hacking services
- **Personal data misuse:** Selling personal data, doxxing

## Sites we review first

<!-- clause:aup-review-first -->
Superior Salon hosts these types only after a person at Superior Salon has reviewed the site, and may decline them:

- **CBD / hemp, supplements, health claims:** Legal but regulated
- **Firearms training or ranges (no sales):** Allowed services, but must not sell weapons
- **Financial advice, crypto:** Regulated; high fraud risk
- **Dating, astrology, psychic and metaphysical services:** Allowed
- **Political or advocacy organizations:** Allowed; checked for hate/harassment

## What you agree to

- You give honest answers about your business during setup.
- You publish only content you have the rights to use.
- You don't use your site to send spam, collect data you are not allowed to collect, or attack other systems.
- You keep your sign-in details private and tell Superior Salon if you think someone else has used them.

## Taking a site down

<!-- clause:takedown-right -->
Superior Salon can suspend or take down a site that breaks this policy. Illegal content or a legal demand means immediate suspension. For any other breach, Superior Salon tells you what is wrong and gives you 3 days to fix it before suspending the site. Every step is logged, and you are told by email and in your dashboard.

A suspended site keeps its data. If the problem is fixed, Superior Salon can restore it.
